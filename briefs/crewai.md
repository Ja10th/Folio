CrewAI
What it is

CrewAI is an open-source framework focused specifically on autonomous multi-agent orchestration, available on GitHub. [3]

• CrewAI provides a role-based implementation of teams of AI agents approach for business stakeholders, as detailed in Getting Started. [3]
• CrewAI Flows: Build event-driven automations that combine precise workflow control, single LLM calls, and native support for Crews. [4]
• Orchestrate start/listen/router steps, manage state, persist execution, and resume long-running workflows. [2]
• CrewAI AMP is designed for enterprises seeking a powerful, reliable solution to transform complex business processes into efficient, intelligent automations. [4]
